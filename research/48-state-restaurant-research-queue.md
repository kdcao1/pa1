## Per-state workflow

1. Find the official entity registry and applicable DBA/assumed-name sources. Use the NASS directory as the starting point: https://www.nass.org/business-services/corporate-registration . Verify each live portal before searching.
2. Search VOLCANO, VULCANO, WOLCANO and relevant close spelling/name variants. Check active and historical records where available. Record search terms, date, record IDs, links, status and limitations. A registry search alone cannot identify every restaurant whose legal company name differs from its public name.
3. Check applicable local DBA records for the restaurant's county/city; identify the jurisdiction before treating a search as coverage. Follow operator aliases disclosed by permits, merchant pages and DBA filings. County sources are checked for discovered premises rather than claiming every county nationwide was exhaustively searched.
4. Match each candidate to an actual restaurant address and meal service using food permits, business/alcohol licenses, restaurant-owned sources and corroborating premises evidence. Corporate/residential/agent addresses alone do not qualify.
5. Record legal operator, company formation, DBA filing, earliest proven restaurant-name use, exact opening if documented, cuisine, sources and outstanding steps separately. Formation/DBA/inspection dates are not automatically opening dates; registered agents are not automatically owners.
6. Deduplicate by premises and operator; screen family-owned locations. Add verified restaurants to the existing sheet. Keep closure/unidentified leads and exclusions in a separate research log.
7. End each batch with an exact resume point. Mark 'Pass completed with limitations' only after business, DBA and restaurant-confirmation steps are documented. 'No qualifying match found in sources searched' never means no restaurant exists anywhere in the state.
